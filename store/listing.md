# Play Store listing copy

Draft text for the Play Console listing. All original — it describes this
app's own games and deliberately names no other product.

---

## App name (30 characters max)

```
Allways Games: Word & Puzzle
```

*28 characters.* Deliberately avoids trademarked game names. "Wordle",
"Connections", "Strands", "Letter Boxed", "Spelling Bee" and "Pips" are
other companies' marks — using them in a listing invites a takedown even
when your own game is original.

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Nine daily puzzles in one app. A new word game, grid and challenge every day.
```

*76 characters.*

> Note: reduce "nine" to the number actually shipping when you publish.
> Overstating what is in the app is both a policy problem and a review
> problem.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
A new set of puzzles every day, in one calm, uncluttered app.

Allways Games brings together the puzzle formats people play daily — word
games, number grids, and lateral-thinking challenges — with a fresh set
waiting for you each morning.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Five — Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Every guess narrows
it down.

Sudoku — The classic nine-by-nine grid, in three difficulties, with pencil
notes and mistake highlighting.

Word Loop — Twelve letters around a square. Chain words end to end and use
every letter, without ever taking two letters from the same side.

Honeycomb — Seven letters, one of them compulsory. Build as many words as
you can and work your way up the ranks.

Tile Match — Clear a layered board by matching free pairs. Simple to learn,
genuinely tricky to finish.

Groups — Sixteen words hide four secret categories of four. The overlaps
are there on purpose.

Weave — Trace themed words through a grid of letters, including the one
long word that crosses it end to end.

BUILT FOR A DAILY HABIT

• A new puzzle in every game, every day
• Streaks and statistics for each game, kept on your device
• Share your result without spoiling the answer
• Play completely offline — no account, no sign-up, no nagging
• Years of puzzles included, so you will not see a repeat

CLEAN AND QUIET

No pop-ups mid-puzzle. No timers pushing you along. Ads appear between
puzzles, never during one — and a single one-off purchase removes them for
good.

Your progress is stored on your phone and nowhere else. There is no
account to make and nothing to sign up for.
```

*Roughly 1,450 characters.*

> Trim any game not shipping in the release. Listing a game that is not in
> the build is a straightforward way to earn one-star reviews.

## Categorisation

- **Category:** Games → Word (or Games → Puzzle)
- **Tags:** word game, puzzle, daily, sudoku, brain training
- **Contains ads:** Yes
- **In-app purchases:** Yes — one product, remove ads
